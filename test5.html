Headline: for the typical clip the model predicts background points as perfectly static (median drift <0.1 cm over 30 frames; 97% of clips within 1 cm in the bg-only/action config). The mean is dominated by a structured tail of ~19 clips, which decomposes into two phenomena:

  • Camera motion / degenerate scenes (night-race 393 cm, bus, tuk-tuk, upside-down, stroller): "static in camera-frame-at-t₀" requires implicit camera-motion estimation; strong ego-motion or far/ambiguous depth breaks it.
  • Chunk-composition drag (goat 73 cm, blackswan 25 cm — mixed config only): co-chunking background points with moving object points can drag the background along; the same points stay pinned in bg-only chunks.
  • Caption ambiguity: a vague "the objects move" caption increases tail drift vs the true action caption (p90 0.44 vs 0.095 cm; bus: 120 cm vs 0.2 cm) — concrete language keeps ambiguous points anchored.
