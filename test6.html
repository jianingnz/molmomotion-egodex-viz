Two observations pending MM: (i) even GT-conditioned generations sit ~0.7 m above the floor — DaS follows conditioning imperfectly, bounding what any trajectory source can achieve; (ii) short-horizon constant-velocity is a strong fidelity baseline over a 2-second window (E3 ≈ G) even though its VBench consistency is clearly worse — fidelity and generation quality measure different failure modes, which is why both tables are reported.
